Questions

before you book.

Bloodrose Tattoos is a custom tattoo studio on North Main Road, Koregaon Park, Pune, with a second studio in Madhapur, Hyderabad. Our artists work in realism, portraits, colour, geometric and mandala tattoos, custom sleeves, spiritual pieces and fine-line work.

Every design is drawn from scratch for you, free, and every session starts with a fresh, sterile setup. Rated 4.9 on Google by 1,500+ clients.

How much does a tattoo cost at Bloodrose Tattoos?

The price depends on size, detail, placement and the hours it takes. Your artist gives you a clear quote at the free consult, before anything is booked. Our current offer is a 12 inch tattoo for ₹5,199.

Is the custom tattoo design really free?

Yes. Your artist draws the design from scratch and refines it with you at no charge. You only pay for the tattoo itself.

Where are your tattoo studios in Pune and Hyderabad?

Our Pune studio is on North Main Road, Ragvilas Society, Liberty Phase 2, Koregaon Park, Pune 411036. Our Hyderabad studio is in Madhapur. Both are open daily from 11 AM to 9:30 PM.

Which artist should I choose?

Sahil is our master of colours, Rohit our master of realism, Amar our master of geometry and mandala, and Omkar our master of portrait with 12 years of experience. Tell us your idea and we will match you with the right artist.

Do you make custom sleeve tattoos?

Yes. We design full, half, quarter and leg sleeves as one connected composition and build them over several sittings. See our custom sleeves page.

How do I book a tattoo appointment?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 75592 88879 or DM @bloodrosetattoos on Instagram with your idea, placement and size. We will set up your free design consult.
