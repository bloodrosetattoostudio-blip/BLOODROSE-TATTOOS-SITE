piercings: faqs.

How much does a piercing cost at Bloodrose Tattoos?

Piercings start at ₹500. The final price depends on the spot and the jewellery you choose, and you'll know it before we start.

Which piercings do you do?

Ear, nose, face and body piercings, including navel and nipple. See the menu above for the full list, or message us to ask about a spot.

Does getting pierced hurt?

It's a quick, sharp pinch that's over in seconds. Lobes are the easiest; cartilage spots like the helix or daith feel a little stronger.

How long does a piercing take to heal?

Lobes usually settle in a couple of months. Cartilage and body piercings take longer, often several months. Your piercer gives you a timeline for your spot.

Do you sell studs and jewellery?

Yes. We sell studs, rings and barbells in the studio, so you can pick your piece on the day or come back to swap once you've healed.

Can I get a tattoo and a piercing on the same visit?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we'll plan both. See our realism and colour tattoos.
